Xiaoxing Hu|胡霄星

I am a Ph.D. student in a joint program between Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai Innovation Institute starting in 2026, advised by Assistant Professor Xue Yang at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my M.S. in Information and Communication Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology, and my B.Eng. in Automation from Central South University. My previous research interests include foundation vision models and their applications in remote sensing, cross-modal representation and alignment, and audio-video understanding and evaluation.

My current research focuses on AI Agents, spanning data pipelines, framework design, evaluation methods, and model optimization.
